# Reference Pools

Use each source for a specific job. Do not treat any gallery as a measure of quality.

## Patterns and flows

| Source | Use it for | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- |

## How to use a reference

1. Screenshot the specific thing, not the whole page.
2. Label the axis: structure, type, color, or motion.
3. Write why it works in one sentence.
4. Write what not to copy.
5. Translate it into a principle you can test.
