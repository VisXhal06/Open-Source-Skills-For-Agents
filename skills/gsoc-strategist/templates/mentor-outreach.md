# Mentor Outreach

## Before sending

- Read the ideas list and the relevant code.
- Make at least one contribution in the area.
- Write down one specific question.
- Check the org's preferred channel.

## Message

```text
Subject: <specific idea> — contribution and one question

Hi <mentor>,

I am preparing for <idea/project> and have been contributing in <area>.
Recent work:
- <PR/issue link>
- <PR/issue link>

I read the idea and the relevant code. My understanding is <one-paragraph summary>.
One thing I cannot determine is <specific question>.

Would <specific approach> fit the project's direction, or is there a constraint
I should account for?

Thanks,
<name>
```

## After sending

- Wait a reasonable period.
- Follow up once with new information, not pressure.
- If there is no response, use the public community channel.
- Keep contributing; do not make your application depend on one reply.
