## Weekly review

- [ ] Add 10–20 candidates
- [ ] Score the top 5
- [ ] Inspect 1–2 deeply
- [ ] Comment on 1 with a reproduction plan
- [ ] Finish 1 PR before starting another
- [ ] Remove stale, assigned, or duplicate issues
- [ ] Update the contribution log for GSoC
